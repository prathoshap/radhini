Student Portal

What we keep, and why

Last updated September 2026

What is stored

  • The dancer's name, and which batch they are in
  • Which adavus and milestones they have completed
  • Assessment scores and the teacher's written notes
  • Practice sessions recorded by the teacher
  • The email address used to sign in

Nothing else. No date of birth, no address, no phone number, no photographs, and no payment details.

Who can see it

Radhini, as the teacher. And the family linked to that dancer — which means a parent, or an older student themselves. Nobody else. One family cannot see another family's records; this is enforced by the database, not only by the website.

Where it lives

On servers in Stockholm, Sweden, run by Supabase. Sign-in emails are sent through Resend, which handles the email address and nothing else. The records themselves never leave the EU.

Signing in

There is no password. Entering your email sends a one-time link that expires after an hour. We never store a password, so one cannot be stolen from us.

Your choices

Ask Radhini and she will show you everything held about your dancer, correct anything wrong, or delete the record entirely. There is no form and no waiting — just ask.

Questions about any of this go to Radhini directly. Contact details are here.
